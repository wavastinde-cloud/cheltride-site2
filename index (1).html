How we work

A clear process protects the creative work.

Our process makes decisions and costs visible. The signed scope—not this overview—defines the parties’ obligations.

01

Listen and diagnose

We discuss the goal, audience, constraints, decision-makers and budget.

02

Define the work

We document deliverables, exclusions, acceptance criteria, review rounds, timing, contributors, fees, external costs, payment and intellectual-property treatment.

03

Confirm and resource

Both parties sign. We confirm the schedule, required inputs, contributor availability and any supplier arrangements.

04

Develop and review

The authorized client representative sends one consolidated response per review round. A new direction or additional deliverable requires a written change order first.

05

Approve and deliver

The client reviews each deliverable against the agreed acceptance criteria. Copyright in eligible final deliverables transfers only after full cleared payment; third-party assets remain subject to their licences.

06

Close and learn

We reconcile approved outside costs, confirm handoff and ask separately whether the client permits public use of the work. A testimonial is optional.

Client and studio responsibilities

Cheltride leads communication, records decisions, coordinates agreed contributors and delivers the scope with reasonable care. The client appoints an authorized approver, supplies accurate materials and permissions, consolidates feedback, and obtains its own legal, regulatory, procurement and cultural approvals unless expressly assigned to Cheltride.

A proposal built around your project

Tell us what you need, your timeline and any budget or procurement requirements. After confirming capacity, we’ll provide a written proposal for the work we can take on, setting out the scope, fee, applicable taxes, known external costs and any agreed allowances. A project begins after the scope is signed and required inputs and resources are confirmed.

How payment works

Fees and payment terms are agreed in the proposal, not published as standard rates. Our proposed starting point is to invoice after the agreed work is delivered, with the due date stated in your agreement; both parties may agree different terms in writing.

If external suppliers are involved, the proposal explains their costs and whether Cheltride or you will contract with them. Supplier terms are confirmed before any commitment, and supplier payments do not automatically follow the client’s payment schedule.

Common questions

Will I know the cost before agreeing?

Yes. Your proposal identifies the fee, applicable taxes and known external costs. Any amount not yet known is identified with its basis or an agreed cap before authorization.

What happens if the scope changes?

We explain the change to the work, fee and schedule in writing. Changed work starts only after both parties approve it.

What if the project is cancelled?

The agreement explains how completed work and approved, non-cancellable costs are reconciled. Any applicable refund or remaining amount due is calculated under the signed terms.
